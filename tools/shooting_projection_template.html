Projected true shooting

2025‑26 season

What a player shot and what he is likely to shoot next are different questions, and they come apart most for exactly the players a front office is deciding about. A rotation big with 180 attempts at 64% is mostly telling you about variance. This projects every player's true shooting by pulling what he shot toward the players who are used the way he is used, by an amount his volume decides — and the amount is fitted out of sample, not chosen.

Where the free throws come from True shooting needs free throws and these pulls carry no box score, but they carry enough to recover one exactly: season points come from the possessions tracking and every made field goal comes from the shot chart, so FTM = POINTS − (2·FGM + FG3M) is arithmetic rather than estimation, and it comes out non‑negative for all 450 players. Turning makes into attempts needs a free throw percentage, and the tracking pulls carry free throws for the fouls they do track — drives, paint, post and elbow touches, about half of a player's trips, shot from the same line by the same player. That percentage is itself pooled toward the league first, for the same reason as everything else here: nine tracked makes should not buy a player a perfect stroke.
