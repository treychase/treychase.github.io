What you are watching One period of tracking, replayed at the speed it happened. Positions are the feed's own, rounded to a tenth of a foot; between samples the skaters are interpolated so the motion is continuous rather than stepped. Velocity is measured over a full second, not frame to frame — over a single frame the rounding alone is worth about 2.5 ft/s, which is enough to pin every skater to the top of the scale and rank nobody. The number inside each skater is the tail of his tracking id, not a sweater number — the feed carries no roster.
